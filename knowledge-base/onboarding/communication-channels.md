# Communication Channels

## Slack Channels
| Channel | Purpose | Who |
|---------|---------|-----|
| #general | Company-wide announcements | Everyone |
| #engineering | Cross-team engineering discussions | All engineers |
| #your-team-name | Team-specific discussions (e.g., #payments-eng) | Your team |
| #incidents | Active incident coordination | On-call + responders |
| #new-joiners | Onboarding support and questions | New hires + buddies |
| #it-helpdesk | IT support requests | Everyone |
| #random | Non-work chat | Everyone |

## Email Distribution Lists
You'll be auto-subscribed to relevant DLs based on your team. See the Access Provisioning Guide for the full list. Key DLs include your team engineering list, team alerts list, and the all-engineering list.

## Meetings
All meetings are scheduled in Outlook and held on Microsoft Teams. Key recurring meetings are added to your calendar during onboarding. See Team Norms for the sprint cadence.

## Documentation
- **Confluence** (`confluence.techcorp.com`): Architecture docs, runbooks, ADRs, and team wikis.
- **GitHub Wikis**: Repository-specific documentation lives alongside the code.
- **SharePoint**: HR policies, company-wide guides, and the onboarding knowledge base.

## Escalation Path
1. Team Slack channel for team-specific issues.
2. Your manager for access, process, or priority questions.
3. #incidents channel for production issues (follow the Incident Response runbook).
4. IT Helpdesk for infrastructure and tooling issues.
